The phone gets answered.

AVXT is an AI receptionist for Ontario home service shops. It picks up when you're mid job or it's 9 PM, asks the questions you'd ask a caller, and rings your phone while they're still on the line.

Worth 15 minutes? how it works

how it works

  1. 01

    Your line forwards to AVXT when nobody picks up, or after hours, you choose. Setup is a code you dial on your own phone, it takes about ten minutes, and I do it with you.

  2. 02

    It answers, asks your qualifying questions, and takes the booking. A caller who passes your rule gets put through to your phone while they're still on the line.

  3. 03

    Every call gets a row in a ledger you can read, the booked ones and the junk. You can check my count against your own phone bill whenever you want.

the price

One flat monthly number, set off your shop's own missed call count, and it goes on the page before you sign. Not per call, not per minute, no overage, and spam never counts.

The price is frozen for a year. The first month is free, and it starts the day the test call passes, not the day you sign.

If you want out, you dial a code on your own line. The forwarding is gone and so is the bill, same day, nobody to argue with.

who this is for

HVAC, plumbing and electrical shops in Ontario where whoever runs the jobs is also the one answering the phone. I set shops up one at a time, myself, and I'd rather do one properly than ten badly.

Not sure your line is even leaking? I'll run a free missed call audit on it, ten minutes of your time, and you see the number before we talk about anything else.
